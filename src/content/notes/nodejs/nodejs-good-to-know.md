# Node.js Good to Know

These topics matter after the core runtime concepts are clear.

## Topics here

- Worker threads.
- Child processes.
- Package versioning.
- Node.js security basics.

## Practical view

Most backend APIs do not need worker threads or child processes every day, but interviews may ask when Node's single-threaded model becomes a limitation.
